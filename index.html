Charlie Geoghegan is a London-based writer, editor and marketer originally from Dublin, Ireland. His wine writing has been published by Decanter, JancisRobinson.com, GuildSomm and the Wine Scholar Guild.

He won the JancisRobinson.com Wine Writing Competition 2026 Judges’ Prize for his short story, Out of time. He was a Fellow at the 2026 Napa Valley Wine Writers’ Symposium and a finalist in the 67 Pall Mall Global Wine Communicator Award for Short-Form Writing in 2025. He holds the WSET Level 4 Diploma in Wine as well as the Italian Wine Scholar and Bordeaux Master-Level qualifications from the Wine Scholar Guild.

Outside of wine, Charlie writes fiction and non-fiction, and his work has been long-listed for the Brick Lane Bookshop Short Story Prize and the Creative Writing Ink Short Story Competition.
